## Project scope to state clearly

CommerceLens is a descriptive analytics prototype. Its RFM groups use fixed rules; category co-occurrence is not proof of causation; and the bundled dataset is illustrative. The prototype does not include authentication, a database, checkout, forecasting, tax, shipping, refunds, or cost-of-goods analysis.
